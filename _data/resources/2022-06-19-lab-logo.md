We finally got a lab logo and icon for putting on your poster presentation
or presentation. This page is the one-stop brand bundle for COCONUT Computer
Club: logo assets, the official color palette, and the philosophy behind the
logo. Lab members who need the assets for posters, slides, merchandise, or
web projects can grab everything from `static/assets/logos`.

Our logo represents COCONUT research and data research that we do at the lab.
It also draws inspiration from binary bits. The curve in the logo represents
movement and the vibrant lab culture that we would like to push for.

## Logo Philosophy

The COCONUT Computer Club logo is a visual manifestation of the club's
values, vision, and mission. By combining the symbolism of its elements, the
logo communicates the club's dedication to creating a collaborative and
inclusive community in the field of information technology and computing.

**1. Colors**

- **Dark Blue:** symbolizes trust, wisdom, stability, and security. It
  represents the reliability and trustworthiness that the club upholds in
  computing.
- **Light Blue:** depicts calmness, peace, and cleanliness, reflecting a
  supportive and soothing atmosphere for learning and collaboration.

**2. Graphic Elements**

- **Coconut with a round dot at the tip:** the coconut is a symbol of
  adaptability, resilience, and diversity, while the round dot at its tip
  represents the peak of achievement or the highest goal the club aims to
  reach.
- **Seven round dots:** depict the seven pillars or fundamental principles of
  the club. Two groups (three and four) of interconnected round dots reflect
  collaboration and connectivity among members, and signify two subgroups or
  two main aspects of the organization: learning and development.
- **Connecting lines:** symbolize relationships and networks among members,
  showing collaboration, communication, and the exchange of ideas within the
  community.

**3. Name: COCONUT**

The name "COCONUT (Computer Club Oriented Network, Utility And Technology)"
reflects the club's inclusiveness, diversity, and adaptability. Just like a
coconut that can grow and mature in various conditions, the club is open to
everyone who wants to learn and contribute, regardless of background and
skill level.

**Conclusion**

The COCONUT Computer Club logo, in its simplicity and complexity,
communicates the spirit, values, and aspirations of the club. It is a symbol
of the club's commitment to pursuing excellence, collaborating, and
contributing to the advancement of computing and information technology.

## The Seven Pillars

1. **Education and Learning:** commitment to providing learning resources and
   developing knowledge in computing and information technology.
2. **Innovation and Creativity:** encouraging creative thinking and the
   development of new ideas in technology and computing.
3. **Collaboration and Community:** building strong relationships among
   members and creating a supportive environment for everyone.
4. **Integrity and Honesty:** maintaining high ethical standards in all
   activities and interactions.
5. **Openness and Inclusivity:** embracing diversity and providing
   opportunities for everyone, regardless of background, race, ethnicity, or
   religion.
6. **Adaptability and Flexibility:** the ability to adapt to technological
   developments and environmental changes.
7. **Leadership and Self-Development:** developing leadership skills and
   nurturing individual potential.
