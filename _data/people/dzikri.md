M. Dzikri Alfauzan N is a cybersecurity enthusiast passionate about network analysis and system security. He holds the CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker) and eMAPT (Mobile Application Penetration Testing) certifications. He enjoys exploring Linux administration, network traffic analysis (Wireshark, tcpdump), secure coding practices, and application security, constantly improving his skills to keep up with the fast-changing world of cybersecurity. His goal is to play a meaningful role in creating secure and reliable systems.

He graduated from Universitas Muhammadiyah Makassar in 2025. At COCONUT, he builds Project VulneraX, an agnostic information security service that includes Ransomware Readiness Assessment.
