Resky Agus is an AI enthusiast and machine learning engineer based in Makassar, South Sulawesi. He works in research and development at PT Maxplus Anugrah Indonesia and is a graduate of Universitas Muhammadiyah Makassar.

He presented the COCONUT bootcamp "YOLO vs DETR: Who Leads the Next Generation of System Detection?", sharing how modern object detection systems can be built with YOLO and DETR, including a real-world case study on traffic congestion analysis using Region of Interest (ROI) and DeepSORT for object tracking.
