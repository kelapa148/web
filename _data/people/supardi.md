Supardi is a database engineer at PT Solusi Pembayaran Elektronik (SPE). He previously worked at PT Docotel Teknologi in 2017 as a system administrator, and he is one of the founding members of COCONUT Computer Club.

His expertise spans databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MariaDB, ClickHouse, TiDB, SQL Server), data engineering (Change Data Capture (CDC), ETL/ELT pipelines, data migration, data integration), observability & AI (PMM, CheckMK, Logstash, LangChain & LangGraph), and infrastructure & DevOps (Linux administration, Docker, CI/CD with GitLab CI / Jenkins, Python, Bash).
